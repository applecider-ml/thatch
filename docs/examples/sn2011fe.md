# SN 2011fe (Type Ia)

SN 2011fe in M101 is the best-studied nearby Type Ia supernova, with 292 HST observations across 25 programs spanning 11.5 years.

## Photometry

Multi-band light curves from pre-explosion imaging through late-time nebular phases.

## Spectroscopy

71 STIS slit spectra (G230LB, G430L, G750L, G140L) spanning days −14 to +3996 relative to B-maximum, plus COS/G130M FUV spectroscopy at +3996 days.
